About the festival

A New Kind of Festival

NebulaPalooza fuses science and art into one unforgettable weekend. By day, explore interactive workshops on rocketry, astrophotography, and satellite engineering. By night, lose yourself in immersive drone choreography and live synthwave performances that pulse with the rhythm of the cosmos.

Designed for families, students, and space enthusiasts — everyone is welcome to the Launchpad.

Highlights

What's On

Music

Live Synthwave & DJ Sets

Aurora Synth, Lumina Beats, Galaxy Groove and 20+ acts perform across three electrifying nights at Letná Park.

Rocketry

Water Rocket Launch Lab

Watch — and build — model rockets using water pressure propulsion, guided by certified aerospace engineers.

Visual

Night Drone Shows

Two nightly performances feature hundreds of synchronized drones forming nebulae and planetary formations above the city.

Science

Astrophysics Talks

Dr. Pavel Horák on Mars missions. Dr. Amara Singh on exoplanets. Prof. Daniel Weiss on black holes. All free to attend.

Art

Cosmic Painting Masterclasses

From glow-in-the-dark galaxy painting to UV watercolor nebulae — create your own cosmic artwork to take home.

Tech

Interactive Technology Fair

VR space travel, astronaut simulators, satellite tracking, and CubeSat model building in the Výstaviště lab halls.
