Headliner · Day 1

Aurora Synth

Synthwave DJ · Letná Park · 4 June 2026

Aurora Synth is a pioneering force in the European synthwave scene, known for blending analog synth textures with cosmic sound design. Her opening set at NebulaPalooza marks the official launch of the festival's musical programme.

Expect retrofuturist arpeggios, pulsing basslines, and visual projections synchronised to her live performance — a perfect gateway into three days of Space meets Sound.

Speaker Spotlight

Black Holes · Day 1 & 2

Prof. Daniel Weiss

Black Hole Specialist · Výstaviště Praha · 4–5 June 2026

Prof. Daniel Weiss is one of Germany's leading theoretical physicists specialising in gravitational singularities and space-time distortion. His research has been published in Nature Astronomy and cited by NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

At NebulaPalooza he leads both the Black Hole Simulation workshop and the keynote talk "Black Holes: The Universe's Dark Giants" — combining hands-on demos with cutting-edge theory for all ages.

Performs at

  • 17:30 · 4 June — Black Hole Simulation (Workshop)
  • 14:00 · 5 June — Black Holes: Dark Giants (Talk)
  • Výstaviště Praha — Hall A

Artist Spotlight

Painting · Day 3

Master Orion

Galaxy Painter · Výstaviště Praha · 6 June 2026

Master Orion is a celebrated cosmic painter whose large-scale galaxy canvases have been exhibited across Prague, Vienna, and Berlin. His signature technique combines acrylic splatter, airbrush gradients, and metallic ink to recreate the depth and luminosity of deep-space photography.

His NebulaPalooza masterclass "Painting the Milky Way" is designed for all skill levels — from first-time painters to experienced artists looking to explore a new cosmic dimension.

Performs at

  • 11:30 · 6 June — Painting the Milky Way
  • Výstaviště Praha — Hall C
