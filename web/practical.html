Getting There & Around

Practical Info

Everything you need for a smooth visit to NebulaPalooza 2026 in Prague, 4–6 June 2026.

Two Locations

Venues

Outdoor

Letná Park — The Launchpad

Letenské sady
Praha 7, 170 00
Czech Republic

The main outdoor stage for all live music, drone light shows, and rocket launch demonstrations. Open-air, with food stalls and seating areas around the main stage.

  • 🚀 Main concert stage
  • 🚀 Water rocket launch zone (safety perimeter applies)
  • 🚀 Drone show viewing area
  • 🚀 Food & beverage vendors
Indoor / Exhibition

Výstaviště Praha — The Lab

Výstaviště 67
Praha 7, 170 00
Czech Republic

The indoor venue hosting all workshops, science talks, painting masterclasses, and the interactive technology fair. Air-conditioned halls with designated zones for each activity type.

  • 🔬 Hall A: Science talks & lectures
  • 🔬 Hall B: Workshops & engineering labs
  • 🔬 Hall C: Art & painting masterclasses
  • 🔬 Hall D: VR zone & technology fair

Between Venues

Letná Park and Výstaviště Praha are approximately 800 metres apart — a comfortable 10-minute walk through the park. A free shuttle bus will also run between them every 20 minutes from 10:00 to 02:00 each day.

Getting There

Transport

Prague Public Transport (PID)

Both venues are well served by Prague's public transport network. A standard single-ride ticket covers trams and the metro.

  • Tram 1, 8, 25, 26 — stop Letenské náměstí (2 min walk to Letná Park)
  • Tram 5, 12, 15, 17 — stop Výstaviště (direct to venue entrance)
  • Metro Line C — Nádraží Holešovice, then tram to either venue
  • Night trams 91, 92 — run until 05:00 for late-night festival-goers

Cycling

Prague's bike-sharing scheme (Rekola) has stations near both venues. Secure bike parking is available at both locations. The festival encourages cycling as the most space-efficient transport option.

Parking

Limited car parking is available at Výstaviště Praha car park (capacity: 400 vehicles, chargeable). Due to the size of the festival, we strongly recommend public transport to avoid delays. No overnight parking permitted.

Where to Stay

Accommodation

Prague offers a wide range of accommodation options within easy reach of the festival venues in Praha 7.

Budget

Hostel & Camping

Several hostels are available in Holešovice and Letná from €20/night. No on-site camping at NebulaPalooza — nearest campsite is 8 km outside the city.

Mid-range

Hotels in Praha 7

Multiple 3–4 star hotels within 1 km of Výstaviště Praha. Book early — June is peak tourist season in Prague.

Premium

Boutique & Central

The Old Town and Vinohrady districts (20 min by tram) offer premium boutique hotels and serviced apartments.

Entry

Tickets

  • ✓ All workshops (subject to capacity)

  • ✗ Does not include adjacent days

€49 / KČ1200 — All 3 Days

Unlimited access to all NebulaPalooza events across all three days (4 June–6 June 2026) at both venues. Best value option.

  • ✓ All workshops (priority booking)
  • ✓ All science talks

  • ✓ Exclusive festival merchandise bag

Payment

Payment can be made online via our secure ticketing platform (credit/debit cards accepted) or in person at the venue box offices (cash and card, for cash, we accept both CZK and EUR). Tickets are non-transferable.

Children & Concessions

Children under 12 enter free when accompanied by a paying adult. Concession prices (students, 65+) available at the venue box office with valid ID/ISIC Card.
